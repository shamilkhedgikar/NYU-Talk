RelWeights Monte Carlo Simulator

Standalone browser app for comparing geographic Geary under first-order queen or rook contiguity with contextual Geary under stacked-support RelWeights. The app reimplements the notebook logic in JavaScript so the support generator, DGPs, and Geary diagnostics can be tuned without rerunning Python.

Simulation scope
Total runs are capped at 300 so the browser remains responsive. If you change any knob, the app will automatically reinitialize before the next run so histories do not mix incompatible parameter settings.
